# TRINETRA — Engineering Handoff

SIH 2026 · Problem Statement 26183 · Blockchain & Cybersecurity · Team 38 (BPIT, GGSIPU)
Snapshot: 11 Sep 2026 · Supersedes the Sep build-spec package where they conflict (see "Precedence").

**Real-Time Identification of Fraud-Linked Cryptocurrency Exchanges from Victim-Reported Wallet Addresses.**
Trace victim USDT forward on TRON to the earliest KYC-regulated exchange deposit address, identify
the exchange behaviourally (deposit addresses are in no public database), and issue a freeze notice
with a confidence score and verifiable transaction hashes.
